# 🚀 Jhattse Business Restaurant Software: Full Feature List

A comprehensive breakdown of the capabilities included in the Jhattse Business ecosystem, designed for modern F&B operations.

---

## 🧾 POS & Billing Operations
* **Touch-Optimized Billing**: High-speed interface designed for Dine-in, Takeaway, and Delivery workflows.
* **Digital Invoicing**: Eco-friendly receipts sent instantly via **WhatsApp** or SMS.
* **Multi-Format Printing**: Support for 2-inch/3-inch thermal printers and standard laser printers.
* **Split Billing**: Easily split checks by seat number or custom amounts for large groups.
* **Discounts & Offers**: Apply flat or percentage-based discounts to specific items or the total bill.
* **Offline Mode**: Continue billing without internet; data auto-syncs when connectivity is restored.

## 🍽️ Order Management
* **Aggregator Hub**: Unified dashboard for **Zomato** and **Swiggy** orders (eliminates "tablet hell").
* **KOT Automation**: Auto-print Kitchen Order Tickets or display them on a **Kitchen Display System (KDS)**.
* **Table Management**: Visual floor map to track occupancy, running orders, and wait times.
* **QR Code Ordering**:
    * **Scan-to-Order**: Contactless digital menu browsing.
    * **Scan-to-Pay**: Integrated UPI payment at the table.

## 📦 Inventory & Stock
* **Recipe Management (BOM)**: Link raw materials to menu items for auto-deduction (e.g., Pizza sale deduces Dough + Cheese).
* **Low Stock Alerts**: Real-time notifications when ingredients breach minimum threshold levels.
* **Wastage Tracking**: Log spills, spoilage, or kitchen errors to keep stock counts accurate.
* **Vendor Management**: Centralized database for suppliers, Purchase Orders (PO), and payout history.
* **Stock Transfer**: Manage inventory movement between Central Kitchen and outlet branches.

## 📢 Marketing & CRM
* **WhatsApp Marketing**: Send bulk promotional campaigns (e.g., "Happy Hour") to your customer base.
* **Loyalty Management**: Track order history and auto-fill customer details for personalized service.
* **Automated Feedback**: Trigger rating requests via WhatsApp immediately after dining.
* **Commission-Free Storefront**: A free, SEO-friendly ordering website for direct customer orders.

## 📊 Analytics & Accounting
* **Live Dashboard**: Real-time metrics on total sales, top-selling items, and staff performance.
* **GST Compliance**: Auto-generate GSTR reports, E-way bills, and E-invoices.
* **Expense Manager**: Track petty cash, salaries, and utility bills directly in the app.
* **Profit & Loss (P&L)**: Automatic calculation of net profit based on Sales vs. COGS/Expenses.
* **Data Export**: Unlimited exports to Excel/CSV/PDF for external accounting.

## 🛠️ Administrative & Technical
* **Multi-Store Management**: "Super Admin" view to control multiple franchise outlets from one login.
* **Role-Based Access**: Granular permissions (e.g., Cashiers can bill, Managers can void/refund).
* **Cross-Platform**: Runs seamlessly on Android tablets, smartphones, Windows PCs, and Web browsers.
* **Cloud Sync**: Secure cloud backup ensuring data accessibility from anywhere.

## 🚚 Delivery & Logistics
* **3rd Party Logistics (3PL)**: Native integration with **Shiprocket**, **Delhivery**, and **Dunzo**.
* **Delivery Tracking**: Share live status updates (Cooking → Out for Delivery) with customers.
