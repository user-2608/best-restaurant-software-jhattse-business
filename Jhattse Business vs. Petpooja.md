## 🌟 Why Jhattse Wins on Core Capabilities

### 🔐 1. Your Data — Truly Yours
Petpooja restricts full access to protect renewals. Jhattse provides:
* **Lifetime Access:** Sales, inventory, and customer reports.
* **Real-time Analytics:** GST & non-GST insights.
* **No Restrictions:** Export to CSV/Excel/JSON anytime.

### 📲 2. WhatsApp-Native Engagement
Eliminate expensive SMS packs with native automation:
* **Auto-Invoicing:** Send bills and payment links directly to WhatsApp.
* **1-Click Broadcasts:** Send "Monsoon Specials" or discounts instantly.
* **Feedback Loops:** Automated post-order rating requests.

### 🌐 3. Modern, Resilient Tech Stack

* **Offline-First:** Continue billing during outages; auto-syncs when online.
* **Cross-Platform:** Web Admin, Mobile Staff App, and Tablet KDS.
* **Aggregator Hub:** Unified queue for Zomato, Swiggy, and Dunzo.
