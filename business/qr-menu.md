## Best Practices

### Design Tips

1. **Keep it scannable:** Place QR codes at eye level
2. **Clear instructions:** Add Scan to view menu text
3. **Backup options:** Keep few paper menus for elderly customers
4. **Lighting:** Ensure good lighting for easy scanning
5. **Size matters:** QR codes should be at least 2x2 inches

### Content Strategy

1. **Professional photos:** Invest in food photography
2. **Compelling descriptions:** Tell the story behind dishes

4. **Update regularly:** Keep seasonal items fresh
5. **Monitor analytics:** Track popular items

## Common Questions Answered

### What if customers do not have smartphones?

Keep a few printed menus as backup. Most customers today have smartphones, but it is good to accommodate everyone.

### Can customers order directly through QR menu?

Yes! With our Table Ordering feature, customers can order and pay directly through the QR menu. This is available in Professional and Enterprise plans.

### How do I handle special requests?

Add a Special Instructions field for each item. Customers can specify modifications like less spicy, no onions, etc.

### Can I show different menus for lunch and dinner?

Absolutely. Set up day-part menus that automatically switch based on time. Breakfast menu appears till 11 AM, lunch till 4 PM, dinner thereafter.

### What about internet connectivity issues?

Our QR menus are optimized for low bandwidth. They load quickly even on 2G networks. Consider having a backup mobile hotspot for critical times.
