### 🧾 Lightning-Fast POS & Billing
- Touch-optimized interface for dine-in, takeaway & delivery
- Split bills by seat or amount
- Digital invoices via WhatsApp/SMS
- Support for 2"/3" thermal & laser printers
- Apply discounts, offers & combo deals instantly

### 🍽️ Smart Order Management
- **Aggregator Hub**: Unified queue for Zomato, Swiggy, Dunzo
- **KOT Automation**: Auto-print to kitchen or KDS display
- **Table Management**: Visual floor map with live occupancy
- **QR Ordering**: Scan-to-order + scan-to-pay at tables

### 📦 Recipe-Based Inventory Control
- Auto-deduct ingredients per sale (BOM management)
- Low-stock alerts & auto-reorder suggestions
- Wastage tracking for accurate stock counts
- Vendor database & purchase order management
- Stock transfer between outlets

### 📢 WhatsApp-First Marketing & CRM
- Broadcast promotions, happy hours & festivals
- Automated feedback requests post-order
- Customer order history & preferences
- Loyalty programs & repeat-customer tracking
- **Free Commission-Free Online Store** for direct orders

### 📊 Real-Time Analytics & Accounting
- Live sales dashboard with top items & staff performance
- Profit & Loss statements (Sales vs COGS)
- Expense tracking: salaries, utilities, petty cash
- GST reports: GSTR-1, e-invoice, e-way bill
- Unlimited exports: Excel, CSV, PDF

### 🛠️ Enterprise-Grade Admin Controls
- Multi-store "Super Admin" view
- Role-based access (Cashier, Manager, Owner)
